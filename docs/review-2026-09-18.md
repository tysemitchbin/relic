## TL;DR

Relic has a genuinely good core idea: your whole life on one map, built from
Strava, GPX, pins, and trips described in plain English. The engineering
underneath is better than the UI suggested. The privacy-radius redaction,
the instant-load cache and the resumable Strava sync are careful work.

But on `main` it didn't yet look or behave like something people would love
or share:

1. **The social layer was a shell.** You could follow people, but **following
   someone changed nothing about your own experience**. The Feed only ever
   showed *your own* activities. There was no way to react, comment, get
   notified, discover people, share a link, or share a trip.
2. **The visual design read as a wireframe.** It had 96 declarations with
   6–9px text, monospace ALL-CAPS on every button, square 1px-bordered boxes,
   unicode glyphs for icons (◈ ▤ ◐ ⚑ ⊞ ⊙), fifteen different button styles,
   and secondary text that failed contrast. It had a *mood* (paper, ink,
   serif) but no *system*.
3. **Several core paths were broken or unreachable:** the map's activity list
   couldn't be opened at all; "+ Add" was unreachable on phones; returning
   users had to re-tick the Terms box to sign in; `?demo` didn't boot; photos
   on public profiles never loaded for other people.

All three are addressed on the branch, without a rewrite and without a build
step. `index.html` is still one file, and every convention is documented in
`CLAUDE.md`.

## 1. Social infrastructure — the biggest gap

### What existed on `main`
`follows` (asymmetric), search by name, follow/unfollow, and a public profile
listing someone's public activities from `activity_public`, plus the
per-activity Public/Private toggle with privacy-radius redaction (excellent,
kept exactly as is).

### Findings

| # | Problem on `main` | Why it matters | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Feed = your own data only** (`buildFeed()` read your own `db`). | The core reason nobody would come back for social reasons. A follow graph with no feed is just a list of names. | ✅ **Following** tab (people you follow, paginated, from `activity_public` + shared stories) and **You** tab |
| S2 | **No interactions** (no kudos, no comments). | Interactions are the reward loop. Without them posting is shouting into a void. | ✅ Kudos ❤ + threaded comments (delete your own, or any on your own activity) |
| S3 | **No notifications.** You never found out someone followed you. | No follow-back moment means the graph never grows. | ✅ Bell with unread count + drawer (new followers with *Follow back*, kudos, comments). Derived from existing tables, so no triggers are needed |
| S4 | **Discovery = exact name search only.** | Nobody knows their friends' Relic names; the People page was a dead end. | ✅ *Suggested for you*: people who follow you, friends-of-friends ("Followed by Kai + 2"), recently active mappers |
| S5 | **No invite/share links.** | Growth happens outside the app (WhatsApp, Instagram). | ✅ Profile/activity/story links (`?u=…&a=…&s=…`), native share sheet on phones, clipboard on desktop. Signed-out visitors see *"Join Relic to follow **Name** and see their map"* and land on that profile after signing up |
| S6 | **Everything defaulted to private, with no bulk switch**, so every profile looked empty to everyone else. | A social network of empty profiles. | ✅ Settings → Sharing: *Make all public* (auto 500 m privacy radius, pins always stay private) / *Make all private*. The share-profile flow warns you if everything's private |
| S7 | **Public profile was a flat list, with no map.** | For a *life-map* app, the killer social feature is "look at my friend's map". | ✅ Profile hero + **interactive map of their public tracks** (click a track → its card), Follow / Follow back / Following / Friends states |
| S8 | **Photos on public profiles never load for others.** The `"public activity photos"` policy (and its storage twin) subqueries `activities`, which non-owners can no longer read, and RLS applies inside policy subqueries. | Silent: owners add photos, friends never see them. | ✅ Fixed in `supabase-social-v2.sql` §3. **You need to run it** |
| S9 | Mutual follows were never surfaced. | You chose follows over friendships, so the UI has to show when it's mutual. | ✅ "Follows you", "Follow back", "Friends" chips |
| S10 | **Stories couldn't be shared at all.** Your wishlist already says *"trips are the shareable unit."* | The most interesting content in the app was the only content you couldn't share. | ✅ `story_public` snapshots: Private/Shared toggle on a story, interleaved into followers' feeds, listed on your public profile, shareable link |
| S11 | No block / report. | App stores require this for user-generated content, and it matters once strangers can comment. | ✅ Report (reuses the `feedback` table), **Block/Unblock** (server-enforced via RLS), **Remove follower**, and a blocked-accounts list in Settings |
| S12 | `profiles`, `follows`, `activity_public` are readable by the **anonymous** role (no `to authenticated`). There's a commented-out line in §5 to lock down `follows`. Your call (the signed-out invite preview needs `profiles` to stay readable) |
| S13 | The follower list signed avatar URLs one row at a time. | 100 followers meant 100 round trips before anything painted. | ✅ Batched `createSignedUrls` + cache |
| S14 | No private accounts / follow requests. | Fine for now, since you chose follows. | → Revisit if users ask |

### Beyond the list: extra social features built
- **Friends' tracks on your map:** a switch in the Layers menu draws everyone
  you follow's public tracks under yours, one colour per person; tap one to
  jump to it.
- **Share as image:** a 1080×1350 card (map, title, stats, branding) from any
  of your activities or stories. It uses the native share sheet on phones
  and downloads on desktop. (Wishlist: "shareable recap cards".)
- **Getting-started checklist** on the feed (six steps with a progress ring),
  plus an **"On this day"** memory card (wishlist).

### Social ideas for next
- **Kudos/comments on stories** (they're activity-only right now, because
  the tables key on `activities`).
- **Weekly digest email** ("3 friends added trips this week") through the
  Resend setup you already have. This is the cheapest re-engagement channel.
- **"New ground"** (wishlist) is your most shareable number: "Alex explored
  14 km of new ground in Lofoten."
- **Co-owned tracks:** when two mutuals have overlapping tracks on the same
  day, suggest "Were you with Sam?".
- **Push notifications** once you're in a native/Capacitor shell. The
  derived-notification design moves to a real table + trigger then.

## 2. Visual design: why it didn't look professional

The palette and the serif gave Relic a real "explorer's journal"
personality, and that's kept. What was missing was a *system*.

| # | Problem | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Illegible type:** 96 declarations at 6–9px (most common label: 7px mono, uppercase, 2px tracking). | ✅ Nothing below 11px; body 14–15px |
| D2 | **Wrong fonts for UI:** monospace everywhere reads as a dev tool; Cormorant Garamond at 13–15px has hairline strokes that blur on Windows. | ✅ **Inter** for UI (tabular numbers for stats), **Fraunces** for headings and the wordmark; mono only for coordinates |
| D3 | **Contrast failed WCAG AA:** muted text was 3.3:1; sidebar text 1.6–2.3:1. | ✅ `--text-2` / `--text-3` tokens ≥ 4.5:1 |
| D4 | **No system:** 15 button classes, 74 inline `style=""` attributes. | ✅ Tokens for colour/radius/shadow/fonts; one pill button language; legacy class names restyled so the JS didn't change |
| D5 | Everything was a 1px-bordered rectangle, so there was no hierarchy. | ✅ Cards with soft elevation, filled inputs, floating map panels |
| D6 | **Unicode glyphs as icons** (your wishlist: the map buttons "don't make sense"). | ✅ One inline SVG icon set, with tooltips and `aria-label`s |
| D7 | Header mixed 8 destinations and actions as identical tabs, with no avatar or notifications. | ✅ Map · Feed · Activities · People, then **+ Add**, 🔔, and an avatar menu |
| D8 | Map controls: six identical squares. | ✅ Activity-list pill; grouped controls; **Layers** popover (Standard / Satellite / 3D terrain / Friends' tracks); Filters with an active-count badge; "Show everything" |
| D9 | `alert()` ×7, `confirm()` ×4. | ✅ Toasts + a styled confirm sheet (none left) |
| D10 | Bare-text empty states. | ✅ Icon + one sentence of why + the button that fixes it, on every list |
| D11 | No favicon/manifest/share meta, so a shared link previewed as a grey box. | ✅ SVG favicon, `manifest.webmanifest`, description + Open Graph tags. → still needs a PNG `og:image` / `apple-touch-icon` (iOS ignores SVG icons) |
| D12 | No keyboard focus styles. | ✅ `:focus-visible` rings |
| D13 | Pins looked like tracks and stayed huge when zoomed out (wishlist). | ✅ Pins scale with zoom; a pin's panel shows the place, not empty stat tiles. → multiple visit dates per pin needs a column (next) |
| D14 | The login screen was a centred wordmark and three inputs with no value proposition. | ✅ Split layout: a brand panel with a drawn map and a one-line pitch, plus the form card |
| D15 | Metric only. | ✅ Kilometres / miles setting (defaults to miles for en-US). → filter-drawer ranges and the Strava stream charts are still metric |

## Two-account test checklist

What I verified: everything in `?demo` (mobile and desktop widths), the
real sign-in screen, and invite-link capture. **Not verified:** anything
that touches your real Supabase, since I didn't run migrations or sign in.
In order:

- [ ] Run `docs/supabase-social-v2.sql`; no errors.
- [ ] **A**: Settings → *Make all public*. **A**: avatar menu → *Invite
      friends* → copy link.
- [ ] Open the link signed out in another browser: the invite banner names
      A; sign up as **B**, and after onboarding B lands on A's profile (with map).
- [ ] **B** follows A → **A**'s bell shows 1 (it polls every 90 s and on tab focus; a reload also does it) → *Follow back* works from the drawer.
- [ ] **B**'s Feed → Following shows A's activities; kudos + a comment on one.
- [ ] **A**'s bell shows the kudos and the comment; tapping them opens the activity.
- [ ] A photo on one of A's public activities is visible to B (the S8 fix).
- [ ] **A** shares a story (story panel → Shared) → it appears in B's feed and on A's profile.
- [ ] **B** → Layers → *Friends' tracks* shows A's tracks on B's map.
- [ ] **A** blocks B → B can no longer follow A or comment (the server rejects it).
- [ ] *Share as image* on a phone opens the share sheet with the PNG.

Relic ("we", "the app") is a personal activity and life-archive app: it stores your movement history, lets you write about it, and optionally lets you share parts of it with people you choose. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to anyone with a Relic account. Relic is operated by [your name / organisation], contactable at [your contact email] for any question about this policy or your data.

A note on health data

Heart rate, calories, and effort scores are widely treated as special-category ("health") data under data protection law, which requires a higher bar than ordinary personal data. Relic only receives this data if you connect Strava, and only for activities that already contain it. Connecting Strava is always your choice — we ask you to confirm explicitly, separately from agreeing to this policy, before pulling in that data (see §4).

3. Why we process it

  • To run the app — your account, activities, notes and photos exist so Relic can function as your archive. (Legal basis: performance of a contract.)
  • To sync from Strava — only if and when you connect your account. (Legal basis: your explicit consent, withdrawable at any time by disconnecting.)
  • To power optional social features — search, following, and public profiles only ever show activities you've explicitly marked public. (Legal basis: your consent, expressed by that per-activity choice.)
  • To improve the app — aggregate usage analytics (PostHog). (Legal basis: consent, requested separately — see §5.)
  • To respond to feedback — anything you send through the in-app feedback form. (Legal basis: legitimate interest in supporting our users.)

5. Analytics & tracking

Relic uses PostHog to understand how the app is used, so we can fix what's broken and improve what isn't. This is off by default; you're asked to opt in, and can change your mind at any time from [Settings/Profile — link to the toggle once built]. Analytics data is tied to your account so we can correlate it with support requests, but it is never sold or shared beyond PostHog.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you disconnect Strava, we delete the connection's access tokens immediately. If you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), everything tied to it — activities, photos, notes, social connections, everything in the tables above — is permanently deleted within the same operation. There is no recovery after that point.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws, you can:

  • Access & export your data — Profile → Export my data gives you a complete JSON copy of everything listed in §2.
  • Correct anything inaccurate — directly, by editing it in the app.
  • Delete your account and everything in it — Profile → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time — disconnect Strava, make an activity private again, or turn off analytics — without affecting the rest of your account.
  • Object or restrict processing, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, by contacting us first at [your contact email].

8. Security

Your data is protected by row-level database security (each account can only ever read or write its own data, with narrow, explicit exceptions for the social features you opt into), encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access-controlled storage for photos.
